Testing Lifestead

How to install Lifestead Test, open it, and tell us what you find.

Thank you for helping. Lifestead Test is a version of the app made for the few people trying it before anyone else. It installs beside any other app, keeps what you put in on your own device, and updates itself. Keep this guide; the parts about updates and renewing come up again later.

The installer and the link that opens it are for you alone. Please do not pass them to anyone or post them anywhere. Each copy only opens on the device it was approved for, and your first name shows faintly on its screens. If somebody else would like to test, send their name to testers@ghostead.com and they can be invited.

1. Installing on an Android phone

The app is not in the Play Store yet, so your phone will ask you to allow it once. The words differ a little between phones; the steps are the same.

  1. Open the link from the invitation on your phone and download the file. It ends in .apk.
  2. When the download finishes, tap it (or open it from the Files app, in Downloads).
  3. The phone says it is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source. Tap Settings.
  4. Turn on Allow from this source (on some phones, Install unknown apps), then go back.
  5. Tap Install.
  6. If Google Play Protect says the app is from an unknown developer, tap More details, then Install anyway. It says this about every app not sent through the Play Store.
  7. When it says the app is installed, tap Open. You can turn the setting from step 4 back off now if you like; updates do not need it.

2. Installing on a Windows computer

  1. Download Lifestead Test Setup from the link in the invitation and open it.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows says this about installers from small makers it does not know yet.
  3. The installer finishes by itself and puts Lifestead Test in the Start menu.

3. Opening it the first time

  1. Lifestead Test shows a short agreement. Read it, then press I Agree.
  2. Write your first and last name and press Ask for Access. Your email app opens with a request ready to go. Press Send. The request carries your name and a code for this device, nothing else.
  3. An approval comes back by email, usually the same day. On the phone, tap the link in it, then Open in Lifestead Test. On a computer, press Copy the Link on the page that opens, then paste it into the box Lifestead Test shows and press Use This Link.
  4. The app opens. Each phone or computer asks for its own approval, so if you use both, ask from each.

4. Telling us what you find

Anything that went wrong, anything confusing, anything worded badly, and anything you wish it did is worth sending. Small things count.

  • Press the small flag at the edge of any screen, or press and hold any band. The report opens with the place you were already filled in.
  • Pick what kind it is: something went wrong, a wish, or something to change. Say it with the microphone or type it.
  • Your email app opens with the report and a picture of the screen. Read it, then press Send. Nothing leaves your device before that.
  • If the app ever closes by itself, the next time it opens it offers to send what happened.

5. Updates

Most updates arrive by themselves: the app fetches them when it opens and uses them the next time it starts. Now and then a change needs a new installer. When that happens, Thank You for Testing Lifestead on Home says so and has a button to get it. Install it over the one you have; everything you put in stays. What Changed on the same band lists what each update brought.

6. Your records

What you put in stays on your phone or computer. Nothing goes to Ghostead or anybody else. Updates are built so that they never remove a record, but a phone can still be lost or broken, so make a backup now and then from Profile > Backup & Restore. If you turn on sync or backups to OneDrive, Lifestead Test uses a folder of its own there.

7. After 90 days

An approval lasts 90 days. From two weeks before it ends, Thank You for Testing Lifestead on Home shows Ask to Renew. Press it, send the email, and open the link in the reply the same way as the first time. If the time does run out, your records stay where they are and open again as soon as more time is approved.
