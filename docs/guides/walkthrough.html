What you're looking at

Every tab and lens below reflects the app's own real, current structure — the same inventory documented in the App Guide. Sample content inside each lens is representative, not a live data pull.

Your Guide walks you through the app, one line at a time. A card on Home opens it: a few sections to start, one more for each part of life they choose, every item done when its record exists. Each part has a guide (1.0.51.7), grouped by when and read Short or Step by step (1.0.51.8). Opening a step shows a Back to Your Guide pill wherever it lands (1.0.51.9), and Walk me through it (1.0.51.10) puts a strip at the top of the screen that says what to tap next, moves on as each screen is reached, and since 1.0.51.11 outlines the very button it names. This frame shows the navigation only; the strip and the outline are drawn by the app.
A tab's screen is empty until you pick a function, as of 2026-09-24. Tap a tab here and the frame goes blank, which is the app behaving correctly. The box at the top naming the tab came out because the corner button on the left was already naming it. Food lost its list of shortcuts in the same pass, since every row on it was already in the My Foods menu. What is left is the background, which is where a person's use of the app is meant to show over time. That growth is designed and not built, and whatever it becomes is made out of what somebody recorded rather than handed over for recording it, so no streak, level or point total appears anywhere in it.
A thing is defined where it lives; Schedules is only its timeline. Since 2026-09-13 Schedules never defines anything. A med is added, edited or paused in Life > My Meds and gets its reminder times through a Schedule it button that lands on Schedules > Meds with that med ready; Upkeep on Life holds each service or document and Schedules > Upkeep lays them out by date; a hands-on session already happened, so it is logged on Signals. Try it: the round button, then Life, then My Meds, then the round button, then Schedules, then Meds.
The TabHub icon picker (Profile → Appearance & Navigation, below) has grown into 5 real, independently collapsible groups: App Icon (1: the Lifestead mark, the out-of-the-box default), Conditions (19, including the "Graves' / Hashimoto's" butterfly, no longer the default), Insects & Other Wildlife (8, led by the Honeybee — no longer the default either), Animals (38 individually illustrated portraits), and Food Builders (1 — a hand-drawn Dessert icon, the first icon in this whole picker that isn't a condition or a photo-cropped creature) — 67 real choices in total.

The 10 tabs

TabHub replaces a persistent bottom bar — one popup shows every tab at once, color-coded by its own jewel tone. The order reads in groups of three after Home: Food / Schedules / Signals is what you do daily, Insights / Trends / Reports is what it tells you, Garden / Life is the wider world. The Digest tab sat between Garden and Life until 2026-09-19, when it was taken apart into lenses on Life, Garden and Food.

Try these first

  • 1 Tap the round button, then Life, then My Meds; then the round button, then Schedules, then Meds — the two halves of one thing: what you take is defined on Life, when you take it lives on Schedules, and a Schedule it button joins them.
  • 2 Tap the round button, then Life, then Conditions, Health Literacy or Earth Matters — the reading library that used to be the Digest tab, 2,254 cited entries across 25 categories, on the same fold bands as everything else. Search Reading searches all of it at once.
  • 3 Tap the round button, then Trends, then Therapy Response — how long an adjustment or a massage held, which needs the opposite direction of reasoning from Pattern Finder: forward from a session rather than backward from a symptom.
  • 4 Tap the round button, then Food, then the corner button — all 12 builders, each now computing the same depth a curated recipe carries and offering a full report before saving.
  • 5 Tap the round button, then Home — four quick ways to log a meal, the app's own answer to its most-named risk: whether anyone logs consistently enough for correlation to mean anything.
  • 6 Tap the round button, then Profile — the shared OneDrive folder, the backup that writes into it, and the TabHub icon picker's own 5 groups.
  • 7 Tap the round button, then Garden, then Horticulture — the cited home-gardening reading, renamed from Gardening so its name says it is about learning, one tap from the zone picker it informs.
  • 8 Tap the round button, then Home, and scroll to the bottom, Library — the purple group under Life, one flip card per reading category in alphabetical order, each wearing the colour of the tab its entry now lives on, so the card itself says where the Read More pill will take you. The entry arrives open, at the top of the screen.
  • 9 Tap the round button, then Home, and open the Your Guide card: a guide for each part of life you choose, and a Walk me through it button on each step that leads you there one tap at a time.
