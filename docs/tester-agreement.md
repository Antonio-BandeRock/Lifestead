# The tester agreement

Drafted 2026-10-31 for tester plan T12, for Tony to review. This is what a
tester reads and agrees to the first time the tester version opens. The
words in the app are in `lib/testerAgreement.ts`, with Spanish in
`lib/translations/es.ts`; change both together. A change a tester should
read again means raising `TESTER_AGREEMENT_VERSION` in `lib/testerAccess.ts`,
which asks every tester to agree again.

Title: Before you start testing

This is a test version of Lifestead, made for you to try before anyone else
can. A few things to agree to first.

1. This copy is for you alone. Please do not pass the app, its installer or
   your activation link to anybody else, or post them anywhere. Each copy
   opens only on the phone or computer it was approved for. If somebody else
   would like to test, send their name to testers@ghostead.com and they can
   be invited.
2. Your records stay on your device. What you put in stays on this phone or
   computer, and in your cloud folder if you turn on sync or backups.
   Nothing is sent to Ghostead or anybody else. A report you send is an email
   you read before it goes.
3. Things will go wrong. It is a test version, so some screens will break,
   some words will be wrong, and some things will change while you use it. It
   is not medical advice and does not replace your doctor or pharmacist.
4. Keep a backup. Updates keep your records, and the app is built so that an
   update never removes any. Even so, make a backup now and then from
   Profile > Backup & Restore, so a lost or broken phone does not take your
   records with it.
5. Access lasts 90 days at a time. Your approval runs for 90 days. Two weeks
   before it ends the app offers to ask for more time with one press. If it
   ends, your records stay where they are and open again once more time is
   approved.
6. Please tell us what you find. Report a Problem is on every screen.
   Anything that went wrong, anything confusing, and anything you wish it did
   is worth sending.

Button: I Agree

## Not in it, on purpose

- No legal terms. This is a plain request between people who know each
  other, not a contract; the store version's terms of use come later.
- Nothing about payment. The tester version has every paid feature on.
